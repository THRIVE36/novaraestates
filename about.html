Our story

Built on trust since 2010

Novara Estates began with a simple belief: buying a home should feel exciting, not overwhelming. Starting as a three-person office in Lekki, we have grown into a trusted agency spanning Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt.

Today our advisors guide buyers, sellers, landlords and investors through every stage of the journey with honest advice, rigorous due diligence and a genuinely personal service.

  • Licensed and fully insured
  • Verified titles on every listing
  • Multilingual advisors: English, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa
